Q)))) What does your day-to-day work look like?

Overall, my day is about ensuring financial accuracy while continuously improving our automation coverage and stability in a fast-paced Agile setup.

Q))))))) Challenge with flaky tests ?
Ans=>
When I initially joined the project, I inherited existing automation for Supplier Invoices and Supplier Credit Memos. The scripts were showing inconsistent behavior — they passed locally but failed intermittently in CI, especially during cross-browser runs.

I struggled at first because the failures were flaky and hard to reproduce, which impacted the team’s trust in automation.

I analyzed the failures and found a few root causes: weak and brittle locators, some hard waits, and limited reusability in the POM classes. I refactored those tests by replacing them with more reliable role-based and test-id locators, improving synchronization, and adding reusable methods in the POM to handle common flows.

After these changes, flaky failures dropped significantly, execution became stable across browsers, and the suite started behaving consistently in CI. This reduced manual regression effort and gave the team confidence to rely on automation before every release.
